Field notes

Isobar is a closed circuit: an organism imagined as an instrument. Ribs, bearings, pipes, and pressure chambers describe one body, from the complete silhouette down to its smallest fittings.

Every view shares a seed and an explicit clock. A window reveals a place in the machine, never a separate machine. Pause anywhere; return to a time; the same moment will be waiting.
